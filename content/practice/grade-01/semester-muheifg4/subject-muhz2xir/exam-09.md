### 001

> [!QUIZ]
> Which of the following is not one of the six strategic business objectives that businesses are seeking to achieve when they invest in information systems?
>
> **A.** Operational excellence
>
> **B.** Improved decision making
>
> **C.** Improved community relations
>
> **D.** Competitive advantage
>
> **E.** Survival

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 다음 중 기업이 정보시스템에 투자할 때 달성하고자 하는 여섯 가지 전략적 비즈니스 목표(strategic business objectives)에 해당하지 않는 것은?
>
> **A.** 운영 우수성
>
> **B.** 의사결정 개선
>
> **C.** 지역사회 관계 개선
>
> **D.** 경쟁우위
>
> **E.** 생존

> [!ANSWER]
> **C. Improved community relations**
>
> The six strategic business objectives are operational excellence; new products, services, and business models; customer and supplier intimacy; improved decision making; competitive advantage; and survival. A, B, D, and E are all on that list. Community relations is not one of the reasons the textbook gives for investing in information systems.
>

> [!ANSWER-TRANSLATION]
> **C. 지역사회 관계 개선(Improved community relations)**
>
> 여섯 가지 전략적 비즈니스 목표는 운영 우수성, 신제품·서비스·비즈니스 모델, 고객·공급업체 친밀성, 의사결정 개선, 경쟁우위, 생존이다. A, B, D, E는 모두 이 목록에 들어 있다. 지역사회 관계 개선은 교재가 제시하는 정보시스템 투자 목적이 아니다.

### 002

> [!QUIZ]
> Verizon Corporation uses a web-based digital dashboard to provide managers with real-time information in order to:
>
> **A.** comply with regulations.
>
> **B.** improve decision making.
>
> **C.** create customer intimacy.
>
> **D.** improve employee morale.
>
> **E.** create supplier intimacy.

> [!TRANSLATION]
> Verizon Corporation이 웹 기반 디지털 대시보드(digital dashboard)로 관리자에게 실시간 정보를 제공하는 목적은?
>
> **A.** 규정을 준수하기 위해
>
> **B.** 의사결정을 개선하기 위해
>
> **C.** 고객 친밀성을 창출하기 위해
>
> **D.** 직원 사기를 높이기 위해
>
> **E.** 공급업체 친밀성을 창출하기 위해

> [!ANSWER]
> **B. improve decision making.**
>
> Verizon's web-based dashboard shows managers real-time data on customer complaints, network performance, and line outages, so they can act on accurate, timely information instead of guesses. That is the textbook's example of improved decision making. A and D are not objectives tied to this case, and C and E concern relationships with customers or suppliers, not managers' decisions.

> Which of the following may lead to competitive advantage? 1. New products, services, and business models 2. Charging less for superior products 3. Responding to customers in real time
>
> **A.** 1 only
>
> **B.** 1 and 2
>
> **C.** 2 and 3
>
> **D.** 1 and 3
>
> **E.** 1, 2, and 3

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 다음 중 경쟁우위(competitive advantage)로 이어질 수 있는 것은? 1. 새로운 제품, 서비스, 비즈니스 모델 2. 우수한 제품을 더 낮은 가격에 제공하는 것 3. 고객에게 실시간으로 대응하는 것

> **B.** 1과 2
>
> **C.** 2와 3
>
> **D.** 1과 3
>
> **E.** 1, 2, 3 모두

> [!ANSWER]
> **E. 1, 2, and 3**
>
> The textbook says competitive advantage comes from doing things better than competitors: offering new products, services, and business models, charging less for superior products, and responding to customers and suppliers in real time. All three listed items appear in that description, so every option that leaves one out (A–D) is incomplete.

### 004

> [!QUIZ]
> A firm that invests in an information system because it is a necessity of doing business does so because it is seeking to achieve which of the following business objectives?
>
> **A.** Operational excellence
>
> **B.** Improved decision making
>
> **C.** Competitive advantage
>
> **D.** Customer intimacy
>
> **E.** Survival

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 사업을 하는 데 반드시 필요하기 때문에 정보시스템에 투자하는 기업은 다음 중 어떤 비즈니스 목표를 달성하려는 것인가?
>
> **A.** 운영 우수성
>
> **B.** 의사결정 개선
>
> **C.** 경쟁우위
>
> **D.** 고객 친밀성
>
> **E.** 생존

> [!ANSWER]
> **E. Survival**
>
> Survival covers investments a firm must make simply to stay in business, either to keep up with industry-level changes (such as Citibank's ATMs) or to meet regulations and reporting requirements. Information technology then becomes a necessity, a cost of doing business. A, B, C, and D are objectives a firm chooses in order to improve or excel, not ones forced on it as a necessity.
>
> **Slide:** [24](/materials/mis-exam-slides/ch01_17ed.pdf#page=24)
>
> *LO 1-1: How are information systems transforming business, and why are they so essential for running and managing a business today? · Difficulty: Moderate* · [Review Ch.1](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-01)

> [!ANSWER-TRANSLATION]
> **E. 생존(Survival)**
>
> 생존(survival)은 사업을 계속하기 위해 반드시 해야 하는 투자로, 업계 수준의 변화(Citibank의 ATM 등)를 따라가거나 규제·보고 의무를 충족하기 위한 것이다. 이때 정보기술은 사업을 하기 위한 필수품, 즉 사업 비용이 된다. A, B, C, D는 더 잘하거나 앞서기 위해 기업이 선택하는 목표이지 필수 조건으로 강요되는 목표가 아니다.
>
> **관련 슬라이드:** [24](/materials/mis-exam-slides/ch01_17ed.pdf#page=24)
>

> The Mandarin Oriental uses computer systems to keep track of guests' preferences in order to:
>
> **A.** Improve flexibility
>
> **B.** Improve decision making
>
> **C.** Increase efficiency
>
> **D.** Enhance customer intimacy
>
> **E.** Achieve operational excellence

> [!TRANSLATION]
> Mandarin Oriental이 컴퓨터 시스템으로 투숙객의 선호를 추적하는 목적은?
>
> **A.** 유연성 향상

>

>
> The Mandarin Oriental records guests' preferences, such as room temperature, check-in time, and favorite TV programs, and uses them to set up rooms automatically and market to its best customers. Knowing and serving customers individually is customer intimacy. B and E (decision making, operational excellence) are other objectives, and A and C are not about understanding guests.

>
> Mandarin Oriental은 실내 온도, 체크인 시간, 즐겨 보는 TV 프로그램 같은 고객 선호를 기록해 객실을 자동으로 맞추고 최우수 고객에게 맞춤 마케팅을 한다. 고객을 개별적으로 이해하고 맞춰 주는 것이 고객 친밀성(customer intimacy)이다. B와 E(의사결정 개선, 운영 우수성)는 다른 목표이고, A와 C는 고객을 이해하는 것과 관련이 없다.

### 006

> All of the following are examples of challenges that required companies to create information systems as a necessity of doing business except:
>
> **A.** Walmart's development of Retail Link.
>
> **B.** Citibank's development of an ATM network.
>
> **C.** the passage of the Toxic Substances Control Act.
>
> **D.** the passage of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act.
>
> **E.** the passage of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act.

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 다음은 모두 기업이 사업 수행의 필수 요건으로서 정보시스템을 구축해야 했던 과제의 예이다. 예외는?

> [!QUIZ]
> Describe the sociotechnical view of management information systems.

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 경영정보시스템에 대한 사회기술적 관점(sociotechnical view)을 설명하라.

> [!ANSWER]
> In the sociotechnical view of management information systems, optimal organizational performance is achieved by jointly optimizing both the social and technical systems used in production. Adopting a sociotechnical system's perspective helps to avoid purely technological approach to information systems. For instance, the fact that information technology is rapidly declining in cost and growing in power does not necessarily or easily translate into productivity enhancement or bottom-line profits. The fact that a firm has recently installed an enterprise-wide financial reporting system does not necessarily mean that it will be used or used effectively. Likewise, the fact that a firm has recently introduced new business procedures and processes does not necessarily mean employees will be more productive in the absence of investments in new information systems to enable those processes.

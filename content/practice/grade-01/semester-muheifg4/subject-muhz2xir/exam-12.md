### 01

> [!QUIZ]
> Questions about who owns the rights to software, music, and other digital creations belong to which moral dimension of the information age?
>
> **A.** Information rights and obligations
>
> **B.** Accountability and control
>
> **C.** Property rights and obligations
>
> **D.** System quality
>
> **E.** Quality of life

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 소프트웨어·음악 같은 디지털 창작물의 권리를 누가 가지는가의 문제는 정보 시대의 어느 도덕적 차원에 속하는가?
>
> **A.** 정보 권리와 의무
>
> **B.** 책임과 통제
>
> **C.** 재산권과 의무
>
> **D.** 시스템 품질
>
> **E.** 삶의 질

> [!ANSWER]
> **C. Property rights and obligations**
>
> The five moral dimensions are information rights, property rights, accountability and control, system quality, and quality of life. Intellectual property issues fall under property rights and obligations.
>
> *LO 4-1 · Difficulty: Moderate* · **Review:** [Review Ch.4](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-05) · [Glossary](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-00)

> [!ANSWER-TRANSLATION]
> **C. 재산권과 의무**
>
> 다섯 도덕적 차원은 정보 권리, 재산권, 책임과 통제, 시스템 품질, 삶의 질이다. 지적재산 문제는 재산권과 의무에 속한다.
>
> *LO 4-1 · 난이도: 보통* · **복습:** [4장 복습](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-05) · [용어 정리집](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-00)

> Which moral dimension covers which values should be preserved in an information society and which institutions should be protected from violation?
>
> **A.** Information rights
>
> **B.** Property rights
>
> **C.** System quality
>
> **D.** Accountability and control
>
> **E.** Quality of life

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 정보 사회에서 어떤 가치를 지키고 어떤 제도를 침해로부터 보호해야 하는가를 다루는 도덕적 차원은?
>
> **A.** 정보 권리
>
> **B.** 재산권
>
> **C.** 시스템 품질
>
> **D.** 책임과 통제
>
> **E.** 삶의 질

> [!ANSWER]
> **E. Quality of life**
>

>
> **C.** FIP
>
> **D.** COPPA
>

### 06

> [!QUIZ]
> ________ means that you accept the potential costs, duties, and obligations for the decisions you make.
>
> **A.** Responsibility
>
> **B.** Liability
>
> **C.** Accountability
>
> **D.** Due process
>
> **E.** Utility

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 자신이 내린 결정의 잠재적 비용·의무·책무를 받아들이는 것을 ________이라 한다.
>
> **A.** 책임(responsibility)
>
> **B.** 법적 책임(liability)
>
> **C.** 책임 추적성(accountability)
>
> **D.** 적법 절차(due process)
>
> **E.** 효용(utility)

> [!ANSWER]
> **A. Responsibility**
>
> Responsibility is individual acceptance of consequences. Accountability is a feature of institutions (mechanisms to determine who acted); liability is a legal feature (laws to recover damages); due process means known laws and the right to appeal.
>
> *LO 4-2 · Difficulty: Moderate* · **Review:** [Review Ch.4](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-05) · [Glossary](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-00)

> [!ANSWER-TRANSLATION]
> **A. 책임(responsibility)**
>
> 책임(responsibility)은 개인이 결과를 받아들이는 것이다. 책임 추적성은 누가 행동했는지 가리는 제도적 장치, 법적 책임은 손해를 배상받게 하는 법, 적법 절차는 알려진 법과 상소할 권리다.

> All of the following are steps in analyzing an ethical issue EXCEPT:
>
> **A.** identifying and describing the facts clearly
>
> **B.** defining the conflict and the higher-order values involved
>
> **C.** identifying the stakeholders
>
> **D.** assigning responsibility and punishment
>
> **E.** identifying the options you can reasonably take and their consequences

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 윤리적 문제를 분석하는 단계가 **아닌** 것은?
>
> **A.** 사실을 명확히 파악하고 기술하기
>

> [!QUIZ]
> A coworker takes office supplies home, saying the loss is tiny. You reply, "If everyone did that, the company would lose a lot." Which ethical principle are you using?
>
> **A.** Utilitarian principle
>
> **B.** Risk aversion principle
>
> **C.** No free lunch rule
>
> **D.** Kant's categorical imperative
>
> **E.** Slippery slope rule

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 동료가 손실이 아주 적다며 사무용품을 집에 가져간다. 당신은 "모두가 그렇게 하면 회사 손실이 커진다"고 답한다. 어떤 윤리 원칙을 쓴 것인가?
>
> **A.** 공리주의 원칙
>
> **B.** 위험 회피 원칙
>
> **C.** 공짜 점심은 없다 원칙
>
> **D.** 칸트의 정언명령
>
> **E.** 미끄러운 비탈길 원칙

> [!ANSWER]
> **D. Kant's categorical imperative**
>
> Kant's categorical imperative: if an action is not right for everyone to take, it is not right for anyone. The slippery slope rule (Descartes' rule of change) is about an action that cannot be taken repeatedly.
>
> *LO 4-2 · Difficulty: Moderate* · **Review:** [Review Ch.4](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-05) · [Glossary](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-00)

> [!ANSWER-TRANSLATION]
> **D. 칸트의 정언명령**
>
> 칸트의 정언명령: 모두가 해서 옳지 않은 행동은 누구에게도 옳지 않다. 미끄러운 비탈길 원칙(데카르트의 변화의 규칙)은 반복해서 할 수 없는 행동에 관한 것이다.
>
> *LO 4-2 · 난이도: 보통* · **복습:** [4장 복습](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-05) · [용어 정리집](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhz2xir/lecture-00)

> [!QUIZ]
> Which rule says that if an action cannot be taken repeatedly, it is not right to take it at all?
>
> **A.** Golden Rule
>
> **B.** Utilitarian principle
>
> **C.** Descartes' rule of change (slippery slope)
>
> **D.** Ethical no-free-lunch rule
>
> **E.** Risk aversion principle

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 반복해서 할 수 없는 행동이라면 한 번도 해서는 안 된다는 원칙은?
>
> **A.** 황금률
>
> **B.** 공리주의 원칙
>
> **C.** 데카르트의 변화의 규칙(미끄러운 비탈길)
>
> **D.** 공짜 점심은 없다 원칙
>
> **E.** 위험 회피 원칙

> [!ANSWER]
> **C. Descartes' rule of change (slippery slope)**
>

## I. Multiple choice

### 001

> [!QUIZ]
> The interaction between information technology and organizations is influenced:
>
> **A.** solely by the decision making of middle and senior managers.
>
> **B.** by the development of new information technologies.
>
> **C.** by many factors, including structure, politics, culture, and environment.
>
> **D.** by two main macroeconomic forces: capital and labor.
>
> **E.** by the rate of growth of the organization.

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 정보기술과 조직 간의 상호작용은 무엇의 영향을 받는가?
>
> **A.** 오로지 중간관리자와 고위관리자의 의사결정에 의해서만
>
> **B.** 새로운 정보기술의 개발에 의해
>
> **C.** 구조, 정치, 문화, 환경을 포함한 많은 요인에 의해
>
> **D.** 자본과 노동이라는 두 가지 주요 거시경제적 힘에 의해
>
> **E.** 조직의 성장 속도에 의해

> [!ANSWER]
> **C. by many factors, including structure, politics, culture, and environment.**
>
> The textbook describes a two-way relationship between organizations and IT that is mediated by many factors: environment, culture, structure, business processes, politics, and management decisions. A is too narrow, because management decisions are only one of these mediating factors. B looks only at the technology side and ignores the organization. D borrows the capital-and-labor inputs of the microeconomic view, which are not mediating factors, and E (growth rate) is not on the list.
>
> **Slide:** [4](/materials/mis-exam-slides/ch03_18ed.pdf#page=4) · [5](/materials/mis-exam-slides/ch03_18ed.pdf#page=5)
>

> [!ANSWER-TRANSLATION]
> **C. 구조, 정치, 문화, 환경을 포함한 많은 요인에 의해**
>
> 교재는 조직과 IT가 양방향 관계(two-way relationship)에 있고, 그 사이를 환경·문화·구조·비즈니스 프로세스·정치·경영 의사결정 같은 많은 요인이 매개한다고 본다. A는 경영 의사결정이 여러 매개 요인 중 하나일 뿐이므로 지나치게 좁다. B는 기술 쪽만 보고 조직을 빠뜨렸다. D는 미시경제적 관점의 투입 요소인 자본과 노동을 끌어온 것으로 매개 요인이 아니며, E(성장 속도)도 목록에 없다.
>

### 002

> [!QUIZ]
> Which of the following statements about organizations is not true?
>
> **A.** An organization is a stable, formal social structure that takes resources from the environment and processes them to produce outputs.
>
> **B.** An organization is a formal, legal entity with internal rules and procedures that must abide by laws.
>
> **C.** An organization is a collection of people and other social elements.
>
> **D.** Informal groups tend to be more stable in terms of longevity and routineness than organizations.
>
> **E.** An organization is a collection of rights, privileges, obligations, and responsibilities delicately balanced over a period of time through conflict and conflict resolution.

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 조직에 관한 다음 설명 중 옳지 않은 것은?
>
> **A.** 조직은 환경에서 자원을 받아들여 처리해 산출물을 만들어 내는 안정적이고 공식적인 사회 구조다.
>
> **B.** 조직은 내부 규칙과 절차를 가지며 법을 준수해야 하는 공식적·법적 실체다.
>
> **C.** 조직은 사람과 그 밖의 사회적 요소들의 집합이다.
>
> **D.** 비공식 집단은 존속 기간과 일상성 측면에서 조직보다 더 안정적인 경향이 있다.
>
> **E.** 조직은 갈등과 갈등 해결을 거쳐 오랜 시간 동안 섬세하게 균형을 이룬 권리·특권·의무·책임의 집합이다.

> [!ANSWER]
> **D. Informal groups tend to be more stable in terms of longevity and routineness than organizations.**
>
> Organizations are more stable than informal groups in terms of longevity and routineness, so D reverses the textbook statement. A and B are the technical (microeconomic) definition, a stable formal social structure and a formal legal entity with internal rules. C describes an organization as a collection of people and social elements, and E is the behavioral definition of rights, privileges, obligations, and responsibilities balanced through conflict and conflict resolution.
>

>

> [!ANSWER-TRANSLATION]
> **D. 비공식 집단은 존속 기간과 일상성 측면에서 조직보다 더 안정적인 경향이 있다.**
>
> 교재에 따르면 존속 기간과 일상성 면에서 더 안정적인 쪽은 비공식 집단이 아니라 조직이므로, D는 내용을 거꾸로 말한 것이다. A와 B는 기술적(미시경제적) 정의로, 안정적이고 공식적인 사회 구조이자 내부 규칙을 가진 공식적·법적 실체라는 설명이다. C는 조직을 사람과 사회적 요소의 집합으로 본 것이고, E는 권리·특권·의무·책임이 갈등과 갈등 해결을 거쳐 균형을 이룬다는 행동적 정의다.

> [!QUIZ]
> According to the ________ definition of organizations, an organization is seen as a means by which capital and labor are transformed by the organization into outputs to the environment.
>
> **A.** microeconomic
>
> **B.** macroeconomic
>
> **C.** sociotechnical
>
> **D.** behavioral
>
> **E.** psychological

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 조직에 대한 ________ 정의에 따르면, 조직은 자본과 노동이 조직에 의해 환경으로 내보낼 산출물로 변환되는 수단으로 여겨진다.
>
> **A.** 미시경제적(microeconomic)
>
> **B.** 거시경제적(macroeconomic)
>
> **C.** 사회기술적(sociotechnical)
>
> **D.** 행동적(behavioral)
>
> **E.** 심리학적(psychological)

> [!ANSWER]
> **A. microeconomic**
>
> The technical, microeconomic definition (Figure 3.2) treats the organization like a production function: capital and labor come in from the environment, the production process turns them into products and services, and outputs go back to the environment. B (macroeconomic) concerns the whole economy rather than a single firm. C and D describe views that stress people and social relationships, and E (psychological) is not one of the textbook's definitions.
>

> [!ANSWER-TRANSLATION]
> **A. 미시경제적(microeconomic)**
>
> 기술적·미시경제적 정의(Figure 3.2)는 조직을 생산함수처럼 본다. 환경에서 자본과 노동이 들어오고, 생산 과정이 이를 제품과 서비스로 바꾸며, 산출물이 다시 환경으로 나간다. B(거시경제적)는 개별 기업이 아니라 경제 전체를 다루는 말이다. C와 D는 사람과 사회적 관계를 강조하는 관점이고, E(심리학적)는 교재의 정의에 없다.

### 004

> [!QUIZ]
> Which of the following statements about the technical view of organizations is not true?
>
> **A.** It focuses on how inputs are combined to create outputs when technology changes are introduced into a company.
>
> **B.** It sees capital and labor as being easily substituted for one another.
>
> **C.** It emphasizes group relationships, values, and structures.
>
> **D.** It sees the organization as a social structure.
>
> **E.** It sees the firm as being infinitely malleable.

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 조직에 대한 기술적 관점(technical view)에 관한 다음 설명 중 옳지 않은 것은?
>
> **A.** 기업에 기술 변화가 도입될 때 투입물이 어떻게 결합되어 산출물을 만드는지에 초점을 둔다.
>
> **B.** 자본과 노동을 서로 쉽게 대체할 수 있는 것으로 본다.
>
> **C.** 집단 관계, 가치, 구조를 강조한다.
>
> **D.** 조직을 사회 구조로 본다.
>
> **E.** 기업을 무한히 변형 가능한(infinitely malleable) 것으로 본다.

> [!ANSWER]
> **C. It emphasizes group relationships, values, and structures.**
>
> Emphasizing group relationships, values, and structures is the behavioral view, not the technical view, so C is the false statement. The technical view focuses on how inputs are combined into outputs when technology changes (A), treats capital and labor as easily substitutable (B), and sees the firm as infinitely malleable (E). D is also part of the technical definition, which calls the organization a stable, formal social structure.

> **C. 집단 관계, 가치, 구조를 강조한다.**
>
> 집단 관계·가치·구조를 강조하는 것은 기술적 관점이 아니라 행동적 관점(behavioral view)이므로 C가 틀린 설명이다. 기술적 관점은 기술이 바뀔 때 투입물이 어떻게 결합되어 산출물이 되는지에 초점을 두고(A), 자본과 노동을 쉽게 대체할 수 있다고 보며(B), 기업을 무한히 변형 가능한 것으로 본다(E). D도 조직을 안정적이고 공식적인 사회 구조로 보는 기술적 정의의 일부다.

> [!QUIZ]
> Which of the following is not a mediating factor that impacts an organization's relationship with information technology?
>
> **A.** Business processes
>
> **B.** Environment
>
> **C.** Structure of the organization
>
> **D.** Agency costs
>
> **E.** Politics

>
> **A.** 비즈니스 프로세스(business processes)
>

>

>

Agency costs (D) belong to agency theory, which explains how IT changes the economics of the firm; they are an effect of IT, not a factor that mediates the organization-IT relationship.

### 006

> [!QUIZ]
> Which of the following statements about business processes is not true?
>
> **A.** Business processes influence the relationship between an organization and information technology.
>
> **B.** Business processes are a collection of standard operating procedures.
>
> **C.** A business firm is a collection of business processes.
>
> **D.** Business processes are usually ensconced in an organization's culture.
>
> **E.** Business processes are typically unaffected by changes in information systems.

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 비즈니스 프로세스(business processes)에 관한 다음 설명 중 옳지 않은 것은?
>
> **A.** 비즈니스 프로세스는 조직과 정보기술 간의 관계에 영향을 미친다.
>
> **B.** 비즈니스 프로세스는 표준 운영 절차(standard operating procedures)의 집합이다.
>
> **C.** 기업은 비즈니스 프로세스의 집합이다.
>
> **D.** 비즈니스 프로세스는 대개 조직 문화 속에 자리 잡고 있다.
>
> **E.** 비즈니스 프로세스는 일반적으로 정보시스템의 변화에 영향을 받지 않는다.

> [!ANSWER]
> **E. Business processes are typically unaffected by changes in information systems.**
>
> A new information system usually has to change existing routines and business processes to deliver its benefits (Figure 3.4), so E is false. A is true because business processes are one of the mediating factors. B and C restate the hierarchy routines (SOPs) form business processes and business processes form the firm. D is true because processes are embedded in the organization's culture.
>

### 007

> [!QUIZ]
> Which of the following technologies disrupted the traditional publishing industry?
>
> **A.** Instant messaging
>
> **B.** email
>
> **C.** Software as a web service
>
> **D.** PCs
>
> **E.** World Wide Web

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 다음 중 전통적인 출판 산업을 뒤흔든(disrupted) 기술은?
>
> **A.** 인스턴트 메시징(instant messaging)
>
> **B.** 이메일(email)
>
> **C.** 웹 서비스형 소프트웨어(software as a web service)
>
> **D.** PC
>
> **E.** 월드와이드웹(World Wide Web)

> [!ANSWER]
> **E. World Wide Web**
>
> The World Wide Web let content be published and distributed online at almost no cost, substituting for printed products and reshaping the publishing industry; the course notes list printed encyclopedias and media among industries the Internet threatened. Instant messaging (A) and email (B) changed communication rather than publishing. Software as a web service (C) affects the software industry, and PCs (D) disrupted typewriters and mainframes rather than publishing.
>

> [!QUIZ]
> How are the behavioral and technical definitions of an organization different?

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 조직에 대한 행동적 정의(behavioral definition)와 기술적 정의(technical definition)는 어떻게 다른가?

> [!ANSWER]
> The behavioral definition of an organization is that it is a collection of rights, privileges, obligations, and responsibilities that is delicately balanced over a period of time through conflict and conflict resolution. The behavioral view emphasizes group relationships, values, and structures. The technical definition sees an organization as an entity that takes inputs from the environment and processes these to create products that are then consumed by the environment, which supplies additional capital and labor as inputs in a feedback loop. The technical view sees capital and labor as interchangeable units, with the ability to rearrange these units at will, whereas the behavioral view sees that rearranging some aspects of the organization, such as an information system, will have important consequences and changes for the organization's other units.
>

### 024

> [!QUIZ]
> Discuss and provide examples of how an organization's culture, politics, and structure can prevent a firm from fully realizing the benefits of a new information system.

> [!TRANSLATION]

For example, an information system might allow students at a university to take self-managed courses, while the university's basic cultural assumptions include the concept that professors are the purveyors of knowledge. An information system, by allowing the distribution of knowledge, may be better used in a company with a flatter organization. A company with a highly stratified hierarchy may have difficulty adjusting its business processes and structures to an information system that does not follow the same business hierarchy of information.

> [!QUIZ]
> What is agency theory? How does information technology enable a firm to reduce agency costs?

> [!TRANSLATION]

> [!ANSWER]
> According to agency theory, the firm is viewed as a "nexus of contracts" among self- interested individuals rather than as a unified, profit-maximizing entity. A principal (owner) employs "agents" (employees) to perform work on his or her behalf. However, agents need constant supervision and management; otherwise, they will tend to pursue their own interests rather than those of the owners. As firms grow in size and scope, agency costs or coordination costs rise because owners must expend more and more effort supervising and managing employees. Information technology, by reducing the costs of acquiring and analyzing information, permits organizations to reduce agency costs because it becomes easier for managers to oversee a greater number of employees. By reducing overall management costs, information technology enables firms to increase revenues while shrinking the number of middle managers and clerical workers.

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 가치사슬 분석(value chain analysis)은 비즈니스 수준에서 정보시스템이 전략적 영향을 미칠 가능성이 가장 큰 특정 활동을 부각하는 데 유용하다. 이 모형을 논하고, 활동들을 구분하며, 모형을 어떻게 적용할 수 있는지 설명하라.

> The value chain model identifies specific, critical leverage points where a firm can use information technology most effectively to enhance its competitive positions. This model views the firm as a series or chain of basic activities that add a margin of value to a firm's products or services. These activities can be categorized as either primary activities or support activities.
>
> • Primary activities are most directly related to the production and distribution of the firm's products and services that create value for the customer. Primary activities include inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, sales and marketing, and service.
>
> • Support activities make the delivery of the primary activities possible and consist of: organization infrastructure (administration and management), human resources (employee recruiting, hiring, and training), technology (improving products and the production process), and procurement (purchasing input). In value chain analysis, you ask at each stage of the value chain, "How can we use information systems to improve operational efficiency and improve customer and supplier intimacy?" The model allows you to critically examine how you perform value-adding activities at each stage and how the business processes might be improved. You can examine how information systems can be used to improve the relationship with customers and with suppliers who are outside the firm value chain, but which belong to the firm's extended value chain, where they are critical to the firm's success. Supply chain management systems that coordinate the flow of resources into the firm, and customer relationship management systems that coordinate sales and support employees with customers are two of the most common systems that result from a business value chain analysis.

> • 지원 활동은 본원적 활동의 수행을 가능하게 하며 조직 인프라(관리·경영), 인적 자원(직원 모집·채용·교육), 기술(제품과 생산 과정 개선), 조달(투입물 구매)로 구성된다. 가치사슬 분석에서는 가치사슬의 각 단계마다 "정보시스템을 어떻게 활용해 운영 효율성을 높이고 고객·공급업체 친밀성을 높일 수 있을까?"라고 묻는다. 이 모형을 통해 각 단계에서 가치를 더하는 활동을 어떻게 수행하고 있는지, 비즈니스 프로세스를 어떻게 개선할 수 있는지를 비판적으로 검토할 수 있다. 또한 기업의 가치사슬 밖에 있지만 기업의 확장된 가치사슬(extended value chain)에 속해 기업의 성공에 결정적인 고객 및 공급업체와의 관계를 개선하는 데 정보시스템을 어떻게 활용할 수 있는지도 검토할 수 있다. 기업 안으로 들어오는 자원의 흐름을 조정하는 공급망 관리(SCM) 시스템과, 판매·지원 직원과 고객을 조정하는 고객관계관리(CRM) 시스템은 비즈니스 가치사슬 분석에서 나오는 가장 흔한 두 시스템이다.

> [!TRANSLATION]

> [!ANSWER]
> Network economics refers to market situations where the economic value being produced depends on the number of people using a product. For certain products and markets, the real economic value comes from the fact that other people use the product. In these situations, "network effects" are at work. In network economics, the cost of adding a participant in the network is negligible, while the gain in value is relatively much larger. The Internet itself is an example of a successful implementation of network economics—the more people participate, the more valuable and essential a commodity it is. Business models that are based on network effects have been highly successful on the Internet, including social networks, software, messaging apps, and on-demand companies like Uber and Airbnb. Network economics also provides strategic benefits to commercial software vendors. The value of their software and complementary software products increases as more people use them, and there is a larger installed base to justify continued use of the product and vendor support. For instance, if a company were to provide a service through the Internet such as a project management application, the costs to the company of adding another user are small (as the software infrastructure or application is already built), and the more users are signed up, the more profit is made.

### 094

> [!QUIZ]
> Why has the Internet made competitive rivalry more intense?

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 인터넷은 왜 경쟁(competitive rivalry)을 더 치열하게 만들었는가?

> [!ANSWER]
> The Internet has made competitive rivalry more intense because Internet technology is based on universal standards that any company can use, making it easier for rivals to compete on price alone and for new competitors to enter the market. Because information is available to everyone, the Internet also raises the bargaining power of customers, who can quickly find the lowest-cost provider on the web, which may dampen profits. The Internet also widens the geographic market, increasing the number of competitors and reducing differences among competitors, and makes it more difficult to sustain operational advantages.

> [!QUIZ]
> Why is it difficult for firms to sustain a competitive advantage?

> [!TRANSLATION]
> 기업이 경쟁우위(competitive advantage)를 유지하기 어려운 이유는 무엇인가?

> [!ANSWER]

Markets, customer expectations, and technology change; globalization has made these changes even more rapid and unpredictable. The Internet can make competitive advantages disappear very quickly because virtually all companies can use this technology. Information systems alone cannot provide an enduring business advantage. Systems originally intended to be strategic frequently become tools for survival, required by every firm to stay in business, or they may inhibit organizations from making the strategic changes essential for future success strategy.

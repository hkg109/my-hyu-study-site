### 02 · 1 point

> [!QUIZ]
> In a classroom study, students in both groups have Internet access. One group spends much of its online time on educational activities, while the other spends substantially more time on entertainment and games. Which term most specifically describes the gap illustrated by this difference in how online time is spent?

### 03 · 1 point

> [!QUIZ]
> Which statement correctly identifies a limitation of Porter's competitive forces model?
>
> **A.** It identifies competitive pressures but does not provide a specific methodology for exactly what a firm should do to achieve competitive advantage.
>
> **B.** It considers only rivalry among existing competitors and excludes customers and suppliers.
>
> **C.** It assumes that new entrants cannot affect an established firm's profitability.

>
> **근거:** 회상 3; [Ch.3][L3] Slide 37, Porter 모형의 한계에 관한 교수님 메모.

### 13 · 1 point

**추정 복원** · 회상 객관식 13; 주제와 “classifying” 단서만 있어 부정형 질문으로 구성

> [!QUIZ]
> Which of the following is NOT a characteristic that makes digital intellectual property different from traditional physical media?
>
> **A.** The ease of making copies.
>
> **B.** The ease of transmitting material through networks.
>
> **C.** The ease of altering digital content.
>
> **D.** The difficulty of classifying software as a literary work, an invention, or another form of intellectual property.
>
> **E.** The ease of establishing the uniqueness of a digital work.

> [!ANSWER]
> **E. The ease of establishing the uniqueness of a digital work.**
>
> 강의는 복제·전송·변경이 **쉽고**, 소프트웨어의 분류와 고유성 판정은 **어렵다**고 설명한다. 따라서 위처럼 구성한 문제에서는 E가 정답이다. D의 “difficulty”를 “ease”로 바꾸면 D 역시 틀린 문장이 된다.
>
> **불확실성:** 실제 시험의 정답은 기억되지 않았다. 기존 테스트뱅크 048번은 compactness를 포함하고 E를 정답으로 삼지만, 여기서는 회상의 classifying 단서를 반영해 D를 교체했다. **E는 이 복원본의 정답이며 실제 시험 정답 확정이 아니다.**
>
> **근거:** 회상 13; [Ch.4 테스트뱅크][TB4] 048; [Ch.4][L4] Slide 28, 디지털 지식재산의 특성.

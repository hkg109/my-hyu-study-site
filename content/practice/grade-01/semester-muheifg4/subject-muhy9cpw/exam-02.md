## I. Multiple choice · 18 points

Choose the one best answer.

### 01 · 1 point

> [!QUIZ]
> Which statement best describes a digital firm?
>
> **A.** A firm that digitally enables key relationships, core processes, and major assets
>
> **B.** A firm that automates every decision
>
> **C.** A firm that never sells physical goods
>
> **D.** A firm that only advertises online
>
> **E.** A firm without employees

> [!ANSWER]
> **A. A firm that digitally enables key relationships, core processes, and major assets**
>
> Digital organization of relationships, processes, and assets matters more than whether the product is physical.
>
> **Source:** R18 · Digital firm; TB 1:18,22 · [Review Ch.1](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhy9cpw/lecture-01)

### 02 · 1 point

> [!QUIZ]
> Customers place orders at night without waiting for the store’s opening hours. Which feature is most directly illustrated?
>
> **A.** Mass customization
>
> **B.** Space shifting
>
> **C.** Business process integration
>
> **D.** Time shifting
>
> **E.** Product differentiation

> [!ANSWER]
> **D. Time shifting**
>
> Time shifting relaxes time constraints; space shifting relaxes location constraints.
>
> **Source:** R18 · Time/space shifting · [Review Ch.1](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhy9cpw/lecture-01)

### 04 · 1 point

> [!QUIZ]
> A firm plans same-day delivery, but its inventory system updates only once per day. What does this illustrate?
>
> **A.** Business goals, processes, and IS capabilities are interdependent
>
> **B.** Business strategy and IS are unrelated
>
> **C.** Buying hardware completes all organizational change
>
> **D.** Every firm must use the same system
>
> **E.** IS affects only accounting

> [!ANSWER]
> **A. Business goals, processes, and IS capabilities are interdependent**
>
> Executing the strategy requires changes to systems and processes; new system capabilities can also change which strategies are feasible.
>
> **Source:** R18 · Interdependence; TB 1:83 · [Review Ch.1](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhy9cpw/lecture-01)

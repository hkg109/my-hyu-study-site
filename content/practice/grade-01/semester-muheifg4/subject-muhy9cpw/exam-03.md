## I. Multiple choice · 18 points

Choose the one best answer.

### 01 · 1 point

> [!QUIZ]
> Which action best applies the sociotechnical perspective to information systems?
>
> **A.** Exclude all employee input
>
> **B.** Buy only the fastest server
>
> **C.** Jointly adjust technology, work, organization, and people
>
> **D.** Never change existing practices
>
> **E.** Treat training as unrelated to systems

> [!ANSWER]
> **C. Jointly adjust technology, work, organization, and people**
>
> Technology and organization must fit one another. Higher technical performance alone does not resolve resistance or process mismatches.
>
> **Source:** R19 · Sociotechnical view; TB 1:90 · [Review Ch.1](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhy9cpw/lecture-01)

### 02 · 1 point

> [!QUIZ]
> Which system is best suited to recording daily orders, payments, and payroll transactions?
>
> **A.** KMS
>
> **B.** DSS
>
> **C.** ESS
>
> **D.** TPS
>
> **E.** MIS

> [!ANSWER]
> **D. TPS**
>
> TPS handles routine recurring transactions and is an important data source for other management systems.
>
> **Source:** R19 · TPS; TB 2:13,84 · [Review Ch.2](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhy9cpw/lecture-02)

### 03 · 1 point

> [!QUIZ]
> Using the textbook’s management-level classification, which statement about DSS is incorrect?
>
> **A.** It can support model-based analysis
>
> **B.** It supports problems without fully predefined solutions
>
> **C.** It is defined exclusively for senior managers and is not used by middle managers
>
> **D.** It supports nonroutine decisions
>
> **E.** It can use internal and external data

> [!ANSWER]

>
> **Source:** R19 · DSS incorrect statement; TB 2:17–20,32–34 · [Review Ch.2](/lectures/grade-01/semester-muheifg4/subject-muhy9cpw/lecture-02)
